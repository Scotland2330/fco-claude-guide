01 · Our Process

Three steps. No wasted motion.

Most AI agencies pitch you tools. We pitch you outcomes. Before a single agent gets built, we spend real time inside your business — understanding how the work actually gets done, and where the compounding waste is hiding.

1

The Audit — we sit inside your operations

We interview the people doing the work, map the flows, watch the handoffs, and identify every process that's repetitive, rule-based, or draining senior time on junior work. You get a written report with prioritised opportunities and estimated ROI for each.

🔍 1 week · Fixed scope
2

The Blueprint — we design what to build first

Not everything worth automating is worth automating first. We rank opportunities by impact, risk, and speed to value, then design the agent architecture — what it does, what data it touches, where humans stay in the loop, and how you measure success.

🧭 1 week · You approve every decision
3

The Build — we deploy agents that actually work

We build the agents inside your stack — connected to the tools you already use, trained on your data, and handed over with documentation your team can maintain. Then we stay for 30 days of tuning and support while your team adopts them.

🛠 2–4 weeks · Handover included
💡
Operations-first, AI-second. We started as an operations consultancy, not an AI shop. That means we won't automate a broken process — we'll fix it first, then build the agent on top. Every deployment is a system you'll still be running in three years.

06 · Common Questions

Things founders ask before we start.

How much does this cost?
The audit is a fixed fee scoped to your business size — typically £3,500–£8,000. Builds are quoted after the audit against expected ROI. We only move forward if the numbers make sense for you.
What happens to our data?
Nothing leaves your environment without your approval. Agents can be deployed on infrastructure you control, with data-handling reviewed against your policies. We sign NDAs and DPAs as standard.
Who owns the agents once they're built?
You do. Every prompt, integration, config file, and piece of documentation is handed over. If we part ways, everything keeps running exactly as it was.
What if an agent gets something wrong?
Every agent we build has a human-in-the-loop review point for anything material — money moving, comms going out, customer decisions. The blueprint stage is where we decide together what's fully autonomous vs. review-first.
How long from first call to a working agent?
Typical timeline is 4–6 weeks from the kickoff call to the first agent in production: one week audit, one week blueprint, two to four weeks build, then 30 days of tuning support alongside your team.
Do we need a technical team to work with you?
No. We handle the build. What we need from you is access to the people doing the work, and someone senior enough to make process decisions. If you have a technical lead, they'll be involved in the handover.
What if the audit shows we don't need AI yet?
We'll tell you. Sometimes the answer is a cleaner process, better tooling, or an SOP before an agent. You keep the report either way — and we'd rather turn work down than build something you'll switch off in six months.
